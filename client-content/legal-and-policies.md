# Visa Convention — Legal Policies & Disclaimers (Draft)

---

## 1. Statutory Immigration & Visa Disclaimer
Visa Convention is an independent private consultancy providing educational advisory, documentation assistance, and travel reservation services. 

- **No Guarantee of Visa Issuance:** Visa Convention does not guarantee visa approval, entry clearance, or specific processing speeds. The granting of any visa, permit, or entry authorization is the exclusive, non-delegable prerogative of the respective national government embassies, consulates, high commissions, and border security authorities.
- **No Job Guarantee:** Visa Convention does not recruit for foreign employers or guarantee overseas job placements. Work visa services are strictly limited to administrative paperwork guidance based on valid, pre-existing employer sponsorships obtained independently by the applicant.
- **Authenticity Responsibility:** Clients are strictly responsible for providing truthful, authentic, and unadulterated documents. Visa Convention rejects fraudulent documentation in accordance with national and international laws.

---

## 2. Privacy Policy Summary
- **Data Collection:** Contact inquiries collect client name, phone number, email address, academic background, and destination preferences solely for evaluating eligibility and providing requested consultations.
- **Compliance:** Data is treated confidentially and handled in alignment with the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) and general international data protection norms.
- **No Third-Party Sale:** Client data is never sold, leased, or distributed to third-party marketing companies.

---

## 3. Terms of Service Summary
- **Consultancy Scope:** Engagement is limited to agreed counseling, profile evaluation, university application coordination, and visa file preparation.
- **Embassy / Government Fees:** All statutory embassy fees, visa application charges, biometrics fees, medical examination costs, and courier fees are paid directly to government or authorized collection centers (e.g., VFS Global) and are strictly non-refundable by Visa Convention.
- **Jurisdiction:** All legal disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Hanumangarh, Rajasthan, India.
