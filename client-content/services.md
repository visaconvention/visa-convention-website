# Visa Convention — Core Services Portfolio (Draft)

> **Regulatory Notice:** Visa Convention acts as a professional education, visa documentation, and travel facilitation consultancy. Final visa approval and entry clearance are determined solely by the respective national embassies, high commissions, and border agencies.

---

## 1. Study Visa Guidance
- **Target Audience:** High school graduates, diploma holders, and university graduates seeking undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma programs abroad.
- **Key Offerings:**
  - One-on-one academic profile assessment and career roadmapping.
  - Course and institution shortlisting across 35+ destination countries.
  - Standardized test guidance (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL, GRE/GMAT).
  - Statement of Purpose (SOP) and Letter of Recommendation (LOR) review.
  - Scholarship application support and tuition fee transfer guidance.
  - Complete visa petition filing, financial documentation audit, and mock visa interview preparation.

---

## 2. MBBS & PhD Abroad Admissions
- **Target Audience:** Medical aspirants aiming for recognized international medical degrees (MBBS/MD) and postgraduate scholars pursuing doctoral research programs.
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Guidance on universities recognized by WHO, National Medical Commission (NMC, India), and international medical councils.
  - English-medium instruction verification across leading medical faculties in Europe, Central Asia, and East Asia.
  - Direct admission guidance with zero capitation or hidden agency fees.
  - PhD research proposal alignment, faculty supervisor identification, and funded assistantship/fellowship application assistance.

---

## 3. Tourist & Visitor Visa Assistance
- **Target Audience:** Leisure travelers, families visiting students abroad, and professionals attending conferences.
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Tailored document checklists based on destination consulate requirements.
  - Detailed day-wise travel itinerary structuring.
  - Hotel booking vouchers and verifiable flight itinerary reservations for visa submission.
  - Financial sponsorship and leave-sanction documentation auditing.
  - Biometric appointment scheduling and interview briefing.

---

## 4. Work Visa Support
- **Target Audience:** Skilled workers, professionals, and sponsored employees holding eligible foreign job offers or eligibility point scores.
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Point-based eligibility assessment (e.g., Canadian Express Entry, Australian Skilled Migration).
  - Guidance on official educational credential assessments (WES, ACS, Engineers Australia).
  - Employer-sponsored work permit filing compliance.
  - Dependent visa and family reunification petitions.
- *Strict Disclosure:* Visa Convention does not sell overseas jobs or charge recruitment placement fees; services are strictly limited to administrative and regulatory visa documentation assistance.

---

## 5. Air Ticket & Flight Reservation Assistance
- **Target Audience:** Departing students, business travelers, tourist groups, and international clients.
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Competitive international flight fare discovery with student baggage allowance optimization.
  - Multi-city and transit layover routing assistance.
  - Genuine reservation hold itineraries for embassy visa submissions.
  - Date change, cancellation, and rebooking management.
- *Strict Rule:* Never generate fake PNRs or fabricated boarding passes; all reservations are secured through authorized travel systems.

---

## 6. Additional Support Services
- Foreign exchange (Forex) card and travel card issuance guidance.
- International student travel insurance and health coverage guidance.
- Pre-departure orientation and airport transfer coordination.
- Overseas student accommodation guidance (on-campus dormitories and vetted private student housing).
