## 4. Single Source of Truth & Fallback Policy

1. **Central Canonical Registry:** All 51 destination countries pull directly from [`src/data/country-registry.ts`](file:///c:/Users/ACER/Desktop/Visa%20MASTER/visa-convention-wesbite/src/data/country-registry.ts) and [`client-content/countries/countries.json`](file:///c:/Users/ACER/Desktop/Visa%20MASTER/visa-convention-wesbite/client-content/countries/countries.json). The same approved primary image is used whether the destination is displayed on the Homepage, Study Abroad Directory, Country Profile, Tourist Visa, or Work Visa.
2. **Accessible High-Contrast Fallback:** For countries where a local approved photograph is not yet assigned, the component renders a high-contrast ISO two-letter flag code badge (`flagCode`) with continent label, preventing broken image links or mismatched stock placeholders.
3. **Office & Staff Photography Rule:** Awaiting authentic client-supplied photographs of the Hanumangarh center and counseling directors before embedding office imagery. No generic stock photos are ever passed off as real Visa Convention staff.
